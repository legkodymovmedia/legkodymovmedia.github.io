The case against
Anatoly Legkodymov

In early 2023, entrepreneur Anatoly Legkodymov was arrested in the U.S. over his company Bitzlato. After a year and a half, a judge ruled in his favor — yet ten days later France issued a new warrant on the same matter.

Being prosecuted twice for the same activity violates legal norms and human rights. He asks for support from people in Russia and France.

Two prosecutions.
One case.

Anatoly Legkodymov was arrested in the U.S. in 2023 over Bitzlato, a crypto marketplace serving Russia, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and Belarus. A judge ruled in his favor after 18 months — then France opened a new case on the same facts.

He now faces up to 20 years, still detained despite two immigration rulings in his favor.
